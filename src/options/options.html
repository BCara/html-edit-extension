AI settings

VibeRevise can suggest rewrites, proofread a document and draft replies to comments using an AI service you already have an account with. It is off until you add a key, and it only ever sends anything when you press a button asking for a suggestion.

Leave as it is unless you use a proxy.

What happens to your text

  • Sent only when you ask. Pressing a rewrite, Proofread or Ask AI button sends the words of the paragraphs involved — plus, for a rewrite, a little of the text either side for context, and for Ask AI the comment thread — straight to the address above. Never the file or its markup, and nothing goes to VibeRevise or anyone else.
  • Nothing changes until you accept. Every answer is shown as a suggestion beside the paragraph. Accepting it is one undo step, and the list of changes marks it as AI-suggested.
  • Your key stays in this browser. It is kept in this Chrome profile on this device, is not synced, and is only read by VibeRevise’s own background worker — never by the pages you edit. Anyone with access to this browser profile could read it, as with any saved password.
  • One address at a time. Saving asks Chrome to let VibeRevise reach only the address above. Changing it or forgetting the key takes that permission back.
  • What the provider does with it is governed by your agreement with them, not by VibeRevise. A local model keeps everything on your own machine.
