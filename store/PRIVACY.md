# Quick Edit — privacy policy

*Last updated 1 October 2026.*

Quick Edit is a browser extension and web app for fixing the words in an HTML
document without disturbing its markup. This policy says what it does with
your information. The short version: almost nothing, and nothing at all unless
you turn the optional AI feature on.

## What stays in your browser

- **Your documents.** Quick Edit reads the document you open and edits it in
  your browser. It never uploads it anywhere of ours — there is nowhere of ours.
  Saving writes the file back to your computer, or to the server the document
  came from if that server accepts it.
- **Your name**, if you type one. It is stored in your browser and written into
  the comments you add to documents you save. It is self-declared, not a
  sign-in.
- **Your Anthropic API key and chosen model**, if you add them. Stored in your
  browser on that device. In the extension the key is held where the pages you
  edit cannot read it.

Clear any of these from Quick Edit's settings, or by removing the extension.

## What leaves your browser, and only if you ask

**AI rewrites are off until you add your own Anthropic API key.** When you
select text and ask for a rewrite, Quick Edit sends that selected text — and,
for context, up to a short passage either side of it — to Anthropic, using your
key. Anthropic's handling of that request is governed by your agreement with
Anthropic; requests are billed to your Anthropic account.

Nothing else is sent anywhere: no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking, no
advertising, no account.

## What we never do

We do not collect, sell, share or use your data. We do not run servers that
receive it. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for any purpose
other than the feature you invoked.
